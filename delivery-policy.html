Delivery Policy

Information about order processing, product delivery, delivery charges and delivery-related support at RUBALIA NETWORK.

Preliminary Policy Notice: This is a draft policy for the website demonstration. Actual delivery services, charges, timelines and procedures must be confirmed with suppliers and delivery partners before accepting real orders.

1. Order Processing

After an order is successfully placed, the order details should be reviewed and verified before processing. Product availability, delivery address and applicable charges may be checked before dispatch.

An order confirmation does not necessarily mean that the product has already been dispatched.

2. Delivery Areas

Delivery availability will depend on the product supplier and the logistics service operating in the customer's area. Available delivery locations should be displayed or confirmed before an order is accepted.

3. Estimated Delivery Time

Estimated delivery time may vary depending on the delivery location, product availability, supplier processing time, weather and logistics conditions.

The applicable estimated delivery period should be communicated during checkout or order confirmation. No fixed delivery period is guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed for that order.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, if applicable, should be displayed to the customer before final order confirmation.

Charges may vary by delivery location, package size, product type and logistics provider. Any additional charge must be communicated transparently before the customer agrees to the order.

5. Correct Delivery Information

Customers should provide accurate delivery information, including:

  • Full recipient name.
  • Active contact number.
  • Complete delivery address.
  • District, area and postal information where applicable.
  • Additional directions when necessary.

Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delivery delays or unsuccessful delivery attempts.

6. Delivery Attempts

The delivery provider may contact the customer to coordinate delivery. Customers should remain reachable using the contact number supplied with the order.

If delivery cannot be completed, the customer may need to coordinate a new delivery attempt. Any additional delivery charge must be disclosed before it is applied.

7. Damaged, Wrong or Missing Products

If a customer receives a damaged, incorrect or incomplete product, they should contact the support team as soon as reasonably possible.

To help investigate the issue, customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order reference number.
  • Clear photographs or video of the issue, where appropriate.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Relevant packaging or delivery information, if available.

The issue will be reviewed under the applicable return and refund policy and relevant consumer protection requirements.

8. Order Cancellation

Customers who wish to cancel an order should contact support as early as possible. Cancellation may depend on whether the order has already been processed or dispatched.

Any cancellation fee or restriction must be disclosed clearly and must comply with applicable law.

9. Returns and Refunds

Product returns, replacements and refunds will be handled according to the applicable return and refund policy, the product's stated conditions and Bangladesh's applicable consumer protection laws.

Customers can contact the Support Center for assistance with a delivery or product issue.

10. Delivery Support

For delivery-related questions, customers should contact RUBALIA NETWORK through the official support channels published on the website.

Please include the order reference number when requesting help. Do not share passwords, PINs, OTPs or other confidential account credentials with anyone claiming to provide delivery support.

11. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when delivery partners, supplier arrangements or applicable requirements change. The current approved version should be published on this page.

Important: Before this policy is used for real customer orders, RUBALIA NETWORK must confirm its delivery partners, actual delivery charges, service areas, delivery timelines, complaint process and legal obligations.
