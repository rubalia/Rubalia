3. Employment Opportunities vs. Independent Sales

A job opportunity and an independent product-selling arrangement are not the same thing.

  • Employment: A formal job with defined responsibilities and agreed employment terms.
  • Independent Sales: A separate commercial arrangement that may involve selling products under agreed conditions.

Any independent sales arrangement must be explained separately. It should not be advertised as a salaried job unless it genuinely meets the applicable employment requirements.

4. Application Process

When an official vacancy is published, the application process should clearly explain:

  • Position title and responsibilities.
  • Required qualifications and experience.
  • Work location and working arrangements.
  • Compensation and employment conditions.
  • Application deadline and contact information.

No application should be treated as successful until the applicant receives an official confirmation.

5. Recruitment Safety

  • Do not pay an unofficial person to obtain a job.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • Verify recruitment messages through official channels.
  • Read employment terms before accepting an offer.
  • Report suspicious recruitment requests to support.
