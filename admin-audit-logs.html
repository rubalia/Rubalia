Audit Log Information

Recommended production safeguards
  • Record login, logout and authentication failures.
  • Record sensitive admin actions with actor and timestamp.
  • Keep payment and withdrawal changes traceable.
  • Store audit records securely on the backend.
  • Restrict log access to authorized administrators.
  • Prevent ordinary admins from editing or deleting audit history.
Production requirement: Audit logs must be generated and protected server-side. Browser-only records are not reliable security evidence.
