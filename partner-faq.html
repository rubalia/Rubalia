Products & Sales

The business concept is based on sourcing genuine products from other companies and reselling them to customers. Actual product categories and approved suppliers must be confirmed before launch.
Partners should follow the official pricing policy. Any permitted discount or pricing flexibility must be clearly defined by the company before partners begin selling.
The final ordering process will be provided after the backend order management system is ready. The current product page is a demonstration and does not submit real orders.
Customers must be treated according to the final approved Return & Refund Policy and applicable consumer protection law. The company must publish the detailed process before accepting real orders.

Team & Network Structure

Unilevel is a proposed team structure in which a partner may directly introduce multiple people, and those people may form additional levels below them. This describes a structural concept, not an authorization to pay commissions.
A 10-level Unilevel structure has been proposed for planning. It is not an active earning plan. Any implementation requires independent legal review and approval.
Any future referral feature must follow approved company policies. Invitations must be voluntary, transparent and must not involve misleading income claims or pressure to join.
No recruitment-only reward is activated or promised in this demo. The business concept is product-focused. Any future compensation must be based on a legally reviewed and clearly documented plan.
