Help Articles

Open Admin User Management to review user records, search accounts and inspect available account information. Real account changes require a properly secured backend and permission checks.
Open Partner Applications from the admin menu. Review submitted information and supporting documents before making a decision. Approval or rejection should only be performed by authorized staff.
Open Product Management and use the product creation interface. Before publishing a real product, verify its name, description, price, stock, images and applicable product information.
Use Order Management to inspect order details and monitor order status. In production, every status change must be validated and saved securely on the server.
Authorized administrators should compare the submitted transaction reference with trusted payment records before approving a payment. Never ask users for PIN, OTP, password, CVV or card security details. The current payment page is only a demo interface.
Review the user's eligible balance, withdrawal amount, account information and applicable policy. A real transfer requires a secure server-side workflow and authorized approval.
The proposed business model illustrates a Unilevel network with up to 10 levels. This is a planning concept only. No commission should be activated until the business model has been reviewed for legal compliance and formally approved.
The business should focus on genuine product sales to real customers. Recruitment-only rewards, guaranteed income claims and misleading earnings promises should not be used.
Different staff members should receive only the permissions needed for their work. Production permissions must be enforced by the backend, not merely hidden or shown in the frontend.

No. The current backup page only demonstrates the interface. Real backups must be generated, stored securely, monitored and periodically tested through backend infrastructure.

Support Guidelines

Before reporting an issue, check the relevant help article and note the page where the problem occurred.

  • Describe the issue clearly.
  • Include the affected module or page.
  • Do not share passwords or OTP codes.
  • Never share API keys or secret credentials.
  • Remove private customer information from screenshots.
