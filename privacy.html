1. Introduction

RUBALIA NETWORK respects the privacy of its customers, members and website visitors.

This preliminary policy explains the categories of personal information that the company may collect, how that information may be used, and the safeguards intended to protect it.

The final policy will apply to the services and website features that are actually launched by the company.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used, the company may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, username, email address and phone number.
  • Profile Information: Profile photo and other details voluntarily provided by the user.
  • Order Information: Purchased products, order references, delivery address and order status.
  • Support Information: Messages and details submitted through customer support.
  • Transaction References: Limited payment confirmation information needed to verify a transaction.
  • Technical Information: Browser type, device information, security logs and website activity where appropriate.

The company should collect only information necessary for a specific and legitimate business purpose.

3. How Information May Be Used

Personal information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing customer or member accounts.
  • Processing and managing genuine product orders.
  • Communicating order updates and important service notices.
  • Responding to customer support requests.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Maintaining accurate business and transaction records.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or required consent.

4. Information Sharing

Personal information should not be sold to unrelated parties. It may be shared only where necessary and legally permitted, including with:

  • Product suppliers and delivery partners, where required to fulfil an order.
  • Technology providers supporting hosting, security or website operations.
  • Payment service providers for authorized transaction processing.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where legally required.

Appropriate safeguards and contractual arrangements should be used when third-party service providers process information.

5. Data Storage and Security

The planned operational platform is intended to use a secure server and database rather than relying on browser LocalStorage for long-term account and transaction records.

Security measures should include, where appropriate:

  • Encrypted connections using HTTPS.
  • Secure password hashing.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Access controls for sensitive customer information.
  • Backups and appropriate recovery procedures.
  • Monitoring for suspicious access and security incidents.

No website or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security. The final policy must describe the safeguards actually implemented.

6. Payment Information

Any future payment system must use appropriate payment channels and security procedures.

  • Users should never be asked to share their banking PIN.
  • Users should never disclose OTP or verification codes.
  • Card CVV and banking passwords must not be collected through support forms.
  • Transaction references should be accessible only to authorized personnel when necessary.
  • Payment records should be retained only as required for legitimate and legal purposes.

The current demonstration website does not process real payments, deposits or withdrawals.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

The operational website may use cookies or similar technologies for login sessions, security, essential functionality and user preferences.

The final policy should explain which cookies are used, their purpose, duration and available user controls.

Any non-essential tracking technology should be used only where permitted by applicable law and required consent rules.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information should be kept only for as long as necessary for its stated purpose, subject to applicable legal, accounting and regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled according to applicable legal requirements.

9. User Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally permitted.
  • Ask questions about how their information is used.
  • Raise concerns about unauthorized processing or disclosure.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is legally available.

A verified procedure for receiving and responding to these requests must be established before the operational launch.

10. Children's Privacy

The company does not intend to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Age requirements and any necessary parental consent procedures must be established before registration is enabled.

11. External Websites

The website may contain links to external websites or third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

Users should review the privacy terms of external services before providing personal information to them.

12. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when services, technology, legal requirements or data practices change.

The final published policy should display its effective date and explain how important changes will be communicated.

13. Contact and Privacy Complaints

Users may use the official support channel to ask questions or raise privacy concerns.

Official company contact details and a designated privacy contact should be published before the website launches.
