Return & Refund Policy

Our preliminary guidelines for product returns, replacements, refunds and customer complaints.

Draft / Demo Notice: This page is a preliminary policy for website development. Actual return conditions, time limits and refund procedures must be finalized before real customer orders are accepted. This policy must comply with applicable Bangladesh consumer protection laws.

1. General Policy

RUBALIA NETWORK aims to provide customers with accurate product information and a transparent purchasing experience. Return, replacement and refund requests will be reviewed according to the product condition, order details, supplier arrangements and applicable laws.

2. Reasons for Return Requests

A customer may contact support if:

  • The delivered product is damaged.
  • The delivered product is different from the ordered product.
  • Parts or items are missing from the package.
  • The product has a verified manufacturing defect.
  • The product does not match an important confirmed order detail.

All requests will be assessed fairly and in accordance with applicable consumer rights.

3. Return Conditions

Where a return is appropriate, customers may be asked to provide the following:

  • Order reference number.
  • Photographs or video showing the reported issue.
  • Product packaging and included accessories, where available.
  • A short explanation of the reason for the return.

Any inspection or packaging requirement must not unlawfully restrict the customer's statutory rights.

4. Items That May Have Special Conditions

Certain products may require special handling because of hygiene, safety, perishability, personalization or supplier restrictions. Any special return condition must be clearly disclosed before purchase and must not override rights provided by applicable law.

5. Return Request Procedure

  • Contact the support team with your order reference.
  • Explain the reason for the return request.
  • Provide relevant evidence if requested.
  • Wait for the support team's instructions.
  • Follow the confirmed return or collection procedure.

Customers should not send products to an unverified address. The approved return instructions should be provided by the support team.

6. Replacement or Exchange

Depending on product availability and the nature of the issue, a verified claim may be resolved through replacement, exchange, repair or another appropriate remedy.

The available solution will be communicated to the customer before it is finalized.

7. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered when a refund is the appropriate resolution under the order terms and applicable law. Examples may include:

  • A confirmed order cancellation eligible for a refund.
  • A verified damaged or incorrect product where refund is appropriate.
  • A product that cannot be supplied after payment has been received.
  • Another situation where a refund is legally required or agreed.

8. Refund Processing

After a refund is approved, the customer should receive confirmation of the approved amount and refund method.

Refund timing depends on the original payment method, payment provider and financial institution. The actual expected processing time must be communicated when the refund is approved.

No refund should be described as completed until the relevant payment provider confirms the transaction.

9. Delivery Charges

Responsibility for delivery or return charges will depend on the reason for the return, the confirmed order terms and applicable law.

Any charge payable by the customer must be explained before the return is arranged. Charges must not be used to remove statutory consumer rights.

10. Order Cancellation

Customers wishing to cancel an order should contact support as early as possible. Cancellation may depend on whether the order has already been processed or dispatched.

If payment has already been collected, any eligible refund will be handled under the applicable refund procedure.

11. Refund Status and Communication

Customers may contact support to ask about the status of a return or refund request. Please provide the order reference number so the team can identify the relevant order.

Do not share passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVV numbers or other confidential payment credentials with anyone.

12. Contact and Complaints

For return, replacement or refund assistance, visit the RUBALIA NETWORK Support Center.

Include your order reference and a clear description of the issue. The support team should communicate the next steps and any information required to review the request.

13. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when product categories, supplier arrangements, payment methods or legal requirements change. The latest approved version should be published on this page.

Before Launch: RUBALIA NETWORK must define actual return eligibility, product-specific exceptions, complaint deadlines, return addresses, refund methods and processing procedures. These details should be reviewed against Bangladesh's applicable consumer protection requirements.
