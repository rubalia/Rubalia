Account & Registration

Visit the Register page and complete the required fields. Real account registration will be available after backend authentication is implemented.
Open My Profile from the member dashboard. Profile editing is currently a frontend demonstration and does not save information to a server.
Use the Forgot Password page. Real password recovery will require secure backend verification and email integration.

Products & Orders

The proposed business focuses on purchasing genuine products from suppliers or other companies and reselling those products to customers.
Browse the Products page and select the products you want. The current interface is a demonstration and does not create real orders.
The planned My Orders page will display order information after the order management backend is connected.
Contact customer support with the order reference and relevant details. The final return and replacement policy will be published before the official launch.

Business & Referral Model

The proposed model is product-focused: the company plans to source genuine products and resell them to customers.
Unilevel is a referral structure in which a member can introduce people directly, while the organization may display multiple levels of referrals. Any compensation arrangement requires separate legal review and approval.
A 10-level Unilevel structure has been discussed as a proposal. It is not an active commission plan. It must not be activated unless it is independently reviewed and confirmed lawful under applicable regulations.
No. RUBALIA NETWORK does not promise guaranteed income, profit or financial returns. Any future lawful compensation would depend on actual qualifying activity and published rules.
