1. Purpose of Agreement

This proposed agreement describes the basic terms under which an eligible individual may apply to become an independent product marketing or sales partner of RUBALIA NETWORK, subject to the company's legal status, approved business policies and applicable law.

The relationship must not be represented as employment, a partnership in the legal sense, a franchise or an agency unless a separate authorized written agreement expressly establishes that relationship.

2. Partner Eligibility

  • The applicant must meet the minimum legal age and eligibility requirements.
  • The applicant must provide accurate information required for lawful onboarding.
  • The applicant must agree to follow approved company policies.
  • The company may verify submitted information where lawful and necessary.
  • Approval is subject to the company's authorized onboarding process.

3. Nature of Partner Relationship

A partner is expected to act independently and responsibly. Unless separately agreed in writing, a partner cannot sign contracts, incur liabilities, make commitments or represent that they have authority to bind the company.

The partner is responsible for complying with applicable tax, licensing, consumer protection and other legal obligations relevant to their activities.

4. Product Sales Responsibilities

  • Promote only products approved for sale by the company.
  • Provide accurate product descriptions and approved claims.
  • Explain the total price and relevant delivery charges before purchase.
  • Do not misrepresent product quality, origin, certification or benefits.
  • Do not create fictitious orders or manipulate sales records.
  • Follow approved order, delivery, return and refund procedures.

5. Customer Protection

The partner must treat customers fairly and respectfully. Customers must receive clear information about products, prices, delivery arrangements, applicable return conditions and available support channels before completing a purchase.

  • No coercion, harassment or misleading advertising.
  • No false scarcity or fabricated testimonials.
  • No unauthorized collection of customer payments.
  • Customer complaints must be directed through approved channels.

6. Pricing, Payments and Orders

Product prices, payment methods, order confirmation procedures and delivery charges must be communicated through official company information. No partner may independently change official prices or claim that an unauthorized payment method is company-approved.

The company must publish a separate approved payment policy before accepting customer payments. Partners must never request a customer's password, PIN, OTP, CVV or other confidential payment credentials.

7. Partner Compensation

No salary, commission, bonus, referral reward or other income is promised or activated by this draft agreement.

If the company proposes a compensation plan in the future, the complete plan must be documented separately, including eligibility, calculation method, payment timing, adjustments, refunds and applicable restrictions. It must receive appropriate legal review and authorization before being offered or implemented.

No partner may promise guaranteed income, fixed returns or risk-free financial results.

8. Marketing and Brand Use

  • Use company names, logos and promotional materials only as authorized.
  • Do not publish false claims about company registration or official approvals.
  • Do not modify official advertisements to create misleading impressions.
  • Do not use third-party intellectual property without permission.
  • Stop using company branding when authorization ends.

9. Privacy and Confidentiality

Each party must handle personal and confidential information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws and the company's approved privacy policy.

  • Use personal information only for authorized purposes.
  • Protect account credentials and confidential business information.
  • Do not disclose customer or partner data without lawful authorization.
  • Report suspected data breaches through the designated support channel.

10. Prohibited Conduct

  • Fraud, impersonation or falsification of records.
  • Misleading product or income claims.
  • Unauthorized collection of joining fees or deposits.
  • Fake accounts, fake referrals or fictitious sales.
  • Harassment, discrimination or abusive conduct.
  • Unauthorized use of company or customer information.
  • Any activity prohibited by applicable law.

11. Complaints and Disputes

The parties should first attempt to resolve genuine concerns through the company's designated complaint process. Any formal dispute resolution procedure, jurisdiction and governing law must be specified in the final lawyer-reviewed agreement.

12. Suspension and Termination

The final agreement must define the circumstances and procedure for suspension or termination, including notice, opportunity to respond where appropriate, outstanding legitimate obligations and treatment of customer orders.

Any restriction or termination must comply with applicable law and the final approved company policy.

13. Amendments

Any change to this agreement must be recorded in writing and communicated through an authorized company process. A material change should not be treated as accepted merely because it appears on a website.

14. Governing Law and Legal Review

The governing law, jurisdiction and any mandatory legal requirements must be confirmed by qualified legal counsel before this document is finalized. The company's legal identity and authority to conduct the proposed activities must also be verified.
