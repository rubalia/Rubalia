Partner Guidelines

A practical guide for responsible product selling, customer service, professional communication and ethical business conduct at RUBALIA NETWORK.

Important Notice: This page contains preliminary guidelines for demonstration and planning. These are not a final partner agreement or legal approval. The company must review and approve all applicable policies before launching partner activities.
🎯

1. Our Partner Principles

Every partner is expected to represent RUBALIA NETWORK honestly, professionally and respectfully. Our business approach should focus on genuine products, real customer value and transparent communication.

  • Be honest in every business interaction.
  • Respect customers, other partners and company representatives.
  • Provide accurate information about products and prices.
  • Follow applicable laws and approved company policies.
  • Never pressure anyone to purchase products or join a network.
🛍️

2. Product Selling Guidelines

Product sales must be genuine transactions. Partners should explain product features accurately and help customers make informed decisions.

01
Accurate Product Information Describe products using approved specifications, prices and product information. Do not invent benefits or guarantees.
02
Transparent Pricing Clearly communicate product prices and any applicable delivery charges or other costs before an order is placed.
03
Genuine Orders Do not create fake orders, manipulate sales records or place orders using another person's identity without authorization.
04
Customer Support Help customers with product questions, order information, delivery concerns and approved return procedures.
🤝

3. Customer Communication

  • Communicate politely and professionally.
  • Explain product details without misleading claims.
  • Respect a customer's decision not to purchase.
  • Do not use spam messages, harassment or unwanted repeated calls.
  • Protect customer contact details and personal information.
  • Never share customer information without a lawful reason or proper authorization.
👥

4. Team & Network Conduct

The proposed partner structure is based on a Unilevel concept. Any future team or level-based program must be separately reviewed, approved and implemented only where legally permitted.

  • Support team members with accurate product and business information.
  • Never force anyone to join, recruit or purchase products.
  • Do not promise guaranteed income or financial success.
  • Do not describe recruitment alone as a source of guaranteed earnings.
  • Do not create fake accounts or manipulate referral information.
  • Any future compensation must follow approved written policies and applicable law.
🚫

5. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are not acceptable under these proposed partner guidelines:

  • Making false or misleading product claims.
  • Promising fixed, guaranteed or risk-free income.
  • Using fake testimonials, fake sales or fabricated results.
  • Collecting money through unauthorized personal accounts while claiming it is an official company payment.
  • Requesting or sharing passwords, PINs, OTPs or confidential payment credentials.
  • Misusing the company name, logo or brand identity.
  • Harassing customers or partners.
  • Breaking applicable laws or approved company policies.
💰

6. Income & Commission Transparency

RUBALIA NETWORK does not guarantee income. Any future commission or incentive program must be documented, transparent and legally reviewed before activation.

Current Demonstration Status

No live commission, partner payout or level-based earning system is activated on this frontend demo.

Future Policy Requirement

Any approved compensation must have clear eligibility, calculation, refund adjustment and payment rules.

  • Never advertise estimated earnings as guaranteed income.
  • Clearly distinguish product revenue from personal income.
  • Disclose relevant conditions and limitations.
  • Do not collect unauthorized joining fees or deposits.
⭐

7. Brand & Marketing Rules

  • Use only company-approved logos, banners and marketing materials.
  • Do not claim to be an official company employee unless authorized.
  • Do not modify company advertisements to create misleading claims.
  • Do not publish false company registration, certification or partnership claims.
  • Obtain permission before using third-party images, videos or trademarks.
  • Clearly identify personal opinions as personal opinions.
🔐

8. Privacy & Account Security

  • Keep your account password confidential.
  • Never ask another partner or customer for their OTP or PIN.
  • Use customer information only for legitimate and authorized purposes.
  • Report suspected account misuse or unauthorized access.
  • Follow the company's approved privacy and data protection policies.
⚖️

9. Complaints & Policy Violations

Customers and partners should be able to report concerns through the company's designated support channel. Reports should be reviewed fairly and confidentially where appropriate.

  • Provide clear details when reporting a concern.
  • Do not retaliate against anyone who makes a genuine complaint.
  • Allow the relevant team to review available evidence.
  • Any corrective action must follow approved company policy and applicable law.

Possible actions, if legally appropriate and supported by an approved policy, may include guidance, correction of misleading content, temporary restriction or termination of partner access.

📋

10. Partner Responsibility Checklist

Before representing RUBALIA NETWORK, make sure you understand these principles:

  • I will provide honest product information.
  • I will respect customers and other partners.
  • I will not promise guaranteed income.
  • I will not create fake orders, accounts or sales records.
  • I will protect confidential customer information.
  • I will follow approved company policies and applicable laws.

Need Help or Have a Question?

If you have questions about partner responsibilities, product sales or these preliminary guidelines, contact the support team.
