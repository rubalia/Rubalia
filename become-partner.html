Become a Partner

Learn about the proposed independent product sales partnership opportunity at RUBALIA NETWORK.

Grow Through Genuine Product Sales

Our proposed partnership concept focuses on product knowledge, customer relationships and responsible sales practices. A partner should create real customer value through genuine products.

Demo Status: Partner registration is not currently active. This page does not create an account, partnership agreement, commission entitlement or payment obligation.

1. What Is a Sales Partner?

An independent sales partner is a person who may promote or sell approved products under a separately agreed commercial arrangement.

A partnership does not automatically create an employment relationship. The exact rights, responsibilities and legal status must be defined in a written agreement.

2. Partner Responsibilities

  • Understand the products being offered.
  • Provide honest and accurate product information.
  • Communicate prices and applicable charges clearly.
  • Respect customer privacy and consumer rights.
  • Avoid false advertising or misleading income claims.
  • Follow approved company policies and applicable laws.

3. Potential Partnership Activities

Product Promotion

Share accurate information about approved products and their features.

Customer Relationships

Help customers understand products and access appropriate support.

Sales Coordination

Coordinate genuine customer orders according to approved procedures.

Responsible Marketing

Use approved promotional material and avoid unsupported claims.

4. Basic Eligibility

Final eligibility requirements will be published before partner registration opens. They may include:

  • Meeting the applicable minimum legal age.
  • Providing accurate identity and contact information.
  • Accepting the approved partner agreement.
  • Following product sales and customer protection policies.
  • Meeting any lawful regulatory requirements.

5. Compensation and Commission

No partner commission, referral reward or income is currently activated through this demonstration page.

Any future compensation arrangement must be based on a clearly documented and legally reviewed plan. Its calculation, eligibility, payment conditions and possible deductions must be explained before a partner accepts the arrangement.

No income amount or business result is guaranteed.

6. Proposed Network Structure

A Unilevel structure with up to ten levels has been discussed as a possible future distribution model.

This is a proposal only. It does not mean that ten levels are currently operational or that any participant is entitled to receive payments from those levels.

The structure must undergo legal review before any compensation feature is implemented.

7. Partnership Is Not a Job Offer

Independent sales partnership and employment are different arrangements.

  • A partner operates under an agreed independent arrangement.
  • An employee works under defined employment terms.
  • Payment and responsibilities must be disclosed accurately.
  • A sales partnership must not be falsely advertised as a salaried job.

See our Careers page for potential employment opportunities.

8. Application Process

When partner registration becomes legally and operationally available, the application process will explain:

  • Eligibility and verification requirements.
  • Partner responsibilities and agreement terms.
  • Approved product categories.
  • Applicable compensation conditions, if any.
  • Customer support and complaint procedures.

No application is being submitted or stored through this page.

9. Prohibited Activities

  • Making false or guaranteed income promises.
  • Misrepresenting product features or prices.
  • Pressuring people to purchase products.
  • Using unauthorized advertising materials.
  • Collecting customer passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • Operating an unauthorized compensation arrangement.

10. Partner Policies

Any future partnership must follow the applicable company agreement and published policies.

  • Terms & Conditions
  • Privacy Policy
  • Legal Disclaimer
  • Return & Refund Policy
